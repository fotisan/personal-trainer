Frequently asked questions

I barely have time. Can this really work for me?

Yes — as long as you can realistically commit to 2–3 focused sessions per week. We build around your calendar, not a perfect world schedule that only works on paper.

Do I have to cut out all carbs, alcohol or eating out?

No. We focus on building a framework you can live with long term. You’ll learn how to manage meals out, drinks and social events without starting over every Monday.

I have injuries. Is this still safe for me?

We always start with a detailed history. If needed, I’ll coordinate with your PT or doctor and we’ll program around limitations so you can train safely and still make progress.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients start feeling better within 2–3 weeks and see visible changes in 6–8 weeks. Long-term results depend on consistency, sleep, stress and how closely you follow the plan.
